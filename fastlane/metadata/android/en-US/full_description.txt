Meditate is a friendly meditation timer that helps you build a
consistent practice — with no accounts, no telemetry, and your
data under your own control.

<b>Timer</b>

* Countdown, stopwatch, and Box Breath modes
* Box Breath: pick a pattern (4-4-4-4, 4-7-8-0, …) or dial in
  each phase, with a calm dot tracing the square as you breathe
* Guided mode: import your own audio files, bells ring when the
  track ends
* Starting bell, interval bells (fixed, periodic, or randomised),
  end bell — sound, vibration, or both, with your own audio and
  custom vibration patterns
* Presets for your regular sits, plus a home-screen widget for
  one-tap starts

<b>Log & Stats</b>

* Date-grouped session log with labels and notes, swipe to
  delete with undo
* Daily-goal ring, 13-week contribution heatmap, bar/line charts,
  per-label totals and insights
* CSV export and import, Insight Timer import

<b>Sync</b>

* Optional sync between your devices via your own Nextcloud —
  also works with the GNOME/Linux version of Meditate, which
  shares the same core
* Offline-first: everything works without a network; sync merges
  changes whenever you're back online

<b>Privacy</b>

Sessions live in a local database on your device. The only
network connection is to the Nextcloud server you configure —
there is no other server, no analytics, and no tracking.
